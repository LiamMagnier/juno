# Meridian pilot — measured results (frozen fixture)

Source: operations analytics export, September 2026.

- Baseline period: 80 requests per day; median response time 12 hours.
- Pilot period: 100 requests per day; median response time 9 hours.
- Baseline errors: 8 of 400 requests.
- Pilot errors: 15 of 500 requests.
- The pilot ran for 5 working days in September 2026.
- There was no random allocation and the two groups differ in customer mix,
  so the comparison is observational, not causal.
