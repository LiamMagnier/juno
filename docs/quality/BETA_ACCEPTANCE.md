# Permissioned beta acceptance

No participants have been recruited and no preference results exist. Use a private
development build after essential gates pass. Start with disposable tasks; important
files and unattended automation require the security/recovery acceptance evidence.

Invite 10–15 consenting participants across developers, students, researchers,
knowledge workers and people less comfortable with technical setup. Provide a clear
development-build/Gatekeeper explanation and a withdrawal path. Recruitment messages,
distribution and telemetry activation require the owner's explicit authorization.
Do not ask participants to disable Gatekeeper globally.

Record one pseudonymous participant code, broad role and task id per observation.
Record setup minutes, approvals understood/confusing, success/partial/failure,
interventions, crashes, reconnect/recovery, output reopening, and whether they would
repeat that workflow in Alevr or their existing product. Ask what made them switch
back and whether Auto's choice/cost explanation helped. Record preference in the
participant's words without extrapolating to all users or treating owner feedback
as independent acceptance.

Do not collect prompts, document contents, credentials, filenames, sensitive paths
or screen recordings by default. Evidence exports should contain only task ids,
coarse timings, action types and outcome counters. Any richer collection needs
specific informed consent, a stated retention period and restricted access.
Telemetry is optional; a local manual form is sufficient for this first beta.

Suggested observation columns:

| Participant | Role | Task id | Setup minutes | Outcome | Interventions | Approval confusion | Crash/recovery | Outputs reopened | Repeat preference | Consent scope |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pending | Pending | Pending | — | Not tested | — | Not tested | Not tested | Not tested | No feedback | Not obtained |

Report denominators, withdrawn/missing observations and failure cases. A meaningful
preference claim needs direct feedback or repeat usage; preparing this instrument
does not satisfy that criterion. Link verified beta task outputs to the workflow
benchmark rather than maintaining a second success-counting system.
