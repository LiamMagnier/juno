# Identity v2 · Best-of matrix

Date: 30 October 2026. Author: the Identity v2 spec author. Status: input to `docs/design/IDENTITY_V2.md`
(the single source of truth). This file records, area by area, the strongest idea in each of the three
directions (d1 Precision, d2 Editorial, d3 Sculpted) and in today's product, the evidence for it, and the
pick. Owner decision: d3 Sculpted is the base; owner instruction the same day: "Keep the best from all and
build the best product."

## How to read it

- **Evidence** quotes the judge panel (`J-owner`, `J-ux`, `J-platform`, verbatim file
  `scratchpad/identity-v2/JUDGES.md`) or an audit (`WA` web audit, `NA` native audit, `PR` principles,
  `LUI`, `MEM`, `RES`, `ART` feature audits) with its section number.
- **Screens** are paths. Prefixes:
  - `ref/` = `docs/design/identity-v2/ref/` (copies of the directions' screenshots made for this spec, 1440 px wide).
  - `shots/` = `docs/design/identity-v2/shots/` (renders made while writing this spec; see IDENTITY_V2 §19).
  - `S/` = `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-juno/fa198b77-5ed6-5d1e-9041-b11ebba8d703/scratchpad/identity-v2/`
    (the directions' full shot sets, prototypes and the audit sheets; session scratchpad, not in git).
- **The pick must fit Sculpted.** Sculpted's identity is three ideas: the Arch (a stone held clear of the
  ground), the depth rule ("lift is earned": only floats, and a card that waits for you, cast a shadow),
  and one motion character (things lift and settle with weight). The last column says how each pick
  serves those three ideas, so the product reads as one object, not a collage.

  `S/d2/shots/sheets/overview-light.png`, d3 `S/d3/shots/contact-desktop-light.png` and

## How the picks become one product, not a collage

1. **One material system.** Every pick uses the same five grounds (base, stage, raised, float, well) in
   one granite hue, one action ink, and two signal colours with one job each. d1's measurement and d2's
   pipeline are invisible in the look; they only make Sculpted's palette hold in every appearance.
2. **One depth rule.** d1's step rail, d2's margin and the needs-you card all sit on the stage at tier 0;
   only the composer (when content passes under it), floats, and a question waiting for you lift. Nothing
   imported from d1 or d2 brings its own shadow, border colour or wash.
3. **One shape language.** Every radius is on one ladder and concentric with its parent; icons use the
   same continuous container corners and flat-cut terminals that echo the arch's flat feet.
4. **One motion personality.** d2's motion rules (instant keyboard menus, scrim never outlives its panel)
   and d1's reduced motion are constraints on d3's four springs, not a second set of curves.
5. **One voice.** d1's numerals and d2's receipt merge into one stamp line written in d3's voice.
