# B2B cold email: three sequences (FR / EN), CNIL-compliant

Drafts. The owner sends them himself, from his own mailbox, in small batches (≤ 30 a day, written to a named person). Nothing here has been sent. This is not legal advice; if in doubt, ask a lawyer before the first batch.

## The rules we follow (France, CNIL)

- **B2B email without prior consent is allowed** in France when the message is **relevant to the recipient's professional role** (e.g. an AI workspace offered to an agency director or an operations lead), the person **was informed** that their address may be used for prospecting, and they can **object simply and free of charge** in every message. (CNIL, « La prospection commerciale par courrier électronique ».)
- **B2C is different**: individuals (including sole traders writing from a personal address about non-professional matters) need **prior consent**. Do not cold email consumers. Ever.
- **Legal basis**: legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Write a short legitimate-interest assessment before the first send (template below) and keep it.
- **Information duty** (GDPR art. 14): if the address was not collected from the person, tell them in the **first** email who you are, why you write, where you found the address, and how to object. Each template does this in its footer.
- **Opt-out**: every email ends with a one-line way to object ("reply STOP" works, plus a link if you have one). Honour it within days, permanently, and keep a suppression list so the person is never contacted again by any sequence.
- **Retention**: delete prospects who never answer after the sequence ends; keep no prospect data longer than 3 years from the last contact (CNIL recommendation).
- **Sources**: use addresses published by the person or the company for professional contact (company website, the person's own public professional page). Do not buy lists of unknown origin and do not scrape platforms whose terms forbid it (LinkedIn does).
- **Other countries**: **Germany and Austria require prior consent even for B2B email** (UWG § 7 in Germany): do not cold email there. Belgium allows B2B email to professional addresses with opt-out but check the rules; the UK (PECR) allows email to corporate subscribers with opt-out. When unsure, don't send.
- **Volume**: low and personal. Cold email at scale damages the domain's deliverability; send from a domain other than the transactional one the product uses for sign-in links.

### Legitimate-interest assessment (fill once, keep on file)
- Purpose: present Alevr to professionals whose role involves producing research, documents or code with AI.
- Necessity: a short, relevant email is the least intrusive way to reach them; no tracking pixels, no link tracking.
- Balancing: business address, role-relevant message, three emails at most, free opt-out in each, data deleted after the sequence.
- Data kept: name, professional email, company, role, source URL, date of each email, opt-out status.

## Tracking (no tracking pixels)
Use a spreadsheet: prospect, source URL, role, sequence, dates sent, replied (y/n), opted out (y/n), signed up (link each sequence with `?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=b2b-a` / `b2b-b` / `b2b-c`; recorded at sign-up once the attribution event proposed in METRICS.md is built). Measure replies and sign-ups, not opens.
