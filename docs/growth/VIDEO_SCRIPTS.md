# Short videos: five scripts (30–45 s)

For X, LinkedIn, Product Hunt, YouTube Shorts and Reels. Record real screens from a demo account with demo data (no real client names, no real people's data). Native screen recording at 60 fps, cursor visible, system UI clean (no notifications). 9:16 crop for Shorts/Reels, 16:9 for X/PH/LinkedIn.

Rules: no stock music with vocals, quiet ambient track at most; captions burned in (most views are muted); show real waiting times, cut them with a visible jump rather than speeding up to fake performance; end card is the Alevr lockup and the production URL. Prices on screen are TTC.

Voice-over lines are EN; FR captions are given for LinkedIn FR.
