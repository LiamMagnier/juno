## Launch-day checklist

Week before
- [ ] Maker account active for 7+ days, profile photo, headline, X/LinkedIn linked.
- [ ] Production URL, sign-up and checkout tested end to end on a fresh account (Free → Lite → Pro).
- [ ] Referral links (`/r/<code>`) working, or remove the mention.
- [ ] Product page drafted and scheduled for **00:01 PT on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday**.
- [ ] Gallery and video uploaded; thumbnail is the Alevr mark on the dark field.
- [ ] List of ~50 people who have used Alevr and asked to hear about the launch (opt-in only).

Launch day (Paris time: 09:01 is 00:01 PT)
- [ ] 09:01 post goes live; post the first comment immediately.
- [ ] 09:15 one message to the opt-in list and personal network: "We're live, feedback welcome." Never ask for upvotes (against PH rules) and never trade upvotes.
- [ ] Reply to every comment within 30 minutes, all day. Answer pricing questions with the exact figures above.
- [ ] Post once on X and LinkedIn (see `LINKEDIN_X.md`, post 10).
- [ ] Watch server load and spend (`METRICS.md`); keep the Free allowance as is.
- [ ] 18:00 and 23:00 Paris: thank-you replies, note recurring objections.

Day after
- [ ] Write down: visits, sign-ups, Free → paid, top three objections.
- [ ] Turn the objections into product tasks or FAQ lines.
