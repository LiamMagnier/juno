Editorial calm.
Type does the work.

One quiet system for Alevr on the web, Mac and iPhone. There are fewer borders and a three-step type ladder. Rows are 36px with a faint tint. Every floating layer uses one material, and the composer is one calm surface. Things fade in and drift 2–4px from where they came from. Everything on this board is live: open the menus, hover into a submenu, send a message, drag the sheet. Turn on Slow motion 4× to inspect each step.

A · FoundationsB · IconsC · ComponentsD · MenusE · Pop-upsF · Composer390 · MobileG · MotionI · Native
  • 01One float. Every menu, select, popover, hover card and toast uses --popover at radius 14, inset 6, with 8px items. That replaces six menu systems.
  • 02Three type steps. Section label 12/500 muted, item name 14 ink, and the description 13 muted under it. Mono only for code and ids.
  • 03Tint, not slab. Hover is ink at 5–6%, instant in and 120ms out. Keyboard adds a 2px inset ring. Selection is a single trailing check.
  • 04The composer is concentric. Every control centre sits 26px from the edges of a 26px corner. The primary disc is always ink.
  • 05Soft motion. Opacity takes 120ms while 2–4px of travel settles over 220ms. Exits take 70ms. Nothing scales from zero, blurs or bounces.
  • 06Glass is for floating chrome only. On Mac and iPhone, Liquid Glass sits on the toolbar, sidebar and composer. It never sits on messages, cards or page buttons.
A

Foundations

spec §A · tokens §H

Warm neutral surfaces with graphite ink. In dark mode, each layer is one lightness step higher and carries a 1px light edge in place of a heavy shadow. The radius values are unchanged; only their use is new, so concentric pairs fall out of the ladder.

B

Icons, redrawn

spec §B

A 24-unit grid with a 3–21 live area. The stroke is set in physical pixels so its weight tracks the text beside it: 1.25px at the 14 optical size, 1.5px at 16 and 20, and 2px for the send arrow only. Caps and joins are round, and container corners are 2.5u or 3u. Fill means "on" and nothing else. Each glyph is centred on Inter's cap height. The metaphors match SF Symbols 1:1, so the native set can be SF.

C

Components

spec §C

Every interactive rectangle is radius 8. Text wells and tracks are 10, with an 8 thumb. Buttons have no shadows. There is one hover tint, one selected fill and one disabled ink. The segmented control, switch, tabs, checkbox and tooltips are live.

D

Menus: one system

spec §D

The specimens show the static anatomy. Each has a 6px inset and 36px rows (52 with a description), one trailing slot, inset separators and destructive items last. The submenu's first row lines up with its trigger. The buttons under each pane open the live menus: hover Add to project to see the 100ms intent and the safe triangle (switch on "Show intent triangle"), or use the arrow keys.

E

Pop-ups

spec §E

There are three modal widths (400, 520 and 880) on a pinned header, body and footer grid. The ✕ is centred on the title line, and Cancel sits before the primary. Destructive confirms have no ✕ and focus Cancel. Toasts and notices are one family, always expanded, and sit above the composer.

F

Composer

spec §F

The landing and the dock are the same surface, at the same width and height, so first send never jumps. The field sits on the reading measure and the tools row is quiet. Nothing dims while a reply streams; only the disc changes. The last composer in each pane is live: type, press + then "Add photos and files", and send.

390

Mobile web (390px)

spec §E3, §F5

The composer docks 8px from the edges with a 44px hit for every control. On a phone, + becomes a bottom sheet with a grabber, medium and large detents and drag-to-dismiss. Drag the open sheet, or tap + on the left phone.

G

Motion, step by step

spec §G · ladder 70/120/160/220/360

Opacity takes 120ms on out-soft and travel takes 220ms on out-calm (0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Exits take the rung below, on ease-in, with half the travel. Keyboard-opened layers appear in 0ms. Under reduced motion the travel is 0 and the fades stay. Each replay drives the live demos in both panes.

I

Native: macOS 26 and iOS 26

spec §I · SwiftUI + Liquid Glass

The information architecture, rows, title band and composer anatomy are the same as the web; each platform renders them natively. Glass sits only on the floating control layer: the system sidebar pane, the toolbar items, the composer card and system sheets. Messages, cards and page controls stay opaque. These mockups approximate Liquid Glass with backdrop-filter here only; product CSS keeps the no-backdrop-filter rule.
